Guarantee

Our first-visit comfort guarantee.

If you don’t feel comfortable after your first visit, you don’t pay for it. Plus a 10-year warranty on implants and 5 years on veneers.

Start risk-free
Will it hurt?

We use modern anaesthetic and guided techniques that keep discomfort low. Conscious sedation is available if you prefer it.

Can I pay monthly?

Yes. 6 to 36 month financing, answered the same day with no set-up fee.

I live abroad. How long does it take?

Most plans finish in 7 to 10 days. We do the consult by video before you travel.

Will I know the price before I start?

Yes. You get the plan and total price in writing after the scan. It does not change during treatment.

Do you work with insurance?

We issue invoices and reports for international insurers, with direct billing for selected providers (sample).

What if I have an emergency?

We see emergencies the same day, Monday to Saturday. Out of hours, message us on WhatsApp.
